<h1 align="left" style="display:flex; align-items:center; gap:15px;">
  Project Akura
</h1>

Our research focuses on building an AI-powered, inclusive, and dyslexia-aware **Sinhala content grading system**.  
We aim to improve accessibility, fairness, and personalized learning for Sinhala-medium students.

## Our Mission
To create intelligent, culturally aligned, and linguistically accurate Sinhala-language educational AI systems that support learners with and without dyslexia.
